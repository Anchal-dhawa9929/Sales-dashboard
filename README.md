Project Headline-
Sales Report Dashboard 2026 (Jan – March)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that visualizes 2026 sales performance across multiple channels,payment methods, and product categories.

1- Short Description:
This project presents a comprehensive 2026 Sales Report built entirely in Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard covers January through March and provides view of net revenue, product-wise quantity sold, channel distribution, payment method breakdown, and daily revenue trends. It was designed to help stakeholders quickly understand business performance using clean visuals and an interactive month filter.
2- Tech Stack:
| Microsoft Power BI Desktop-Dashboard creation & data visualization 

| Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)-Data storage & preprocessing

| Power Query-Data cleaning & transformation

| KPI calculations & measures

3- Data Source:
Data was manually sourced from the internet and exported into a structured Excel (.xlsx) file
The dataset includes fields such as: Date, ProductName, NetRevenue, Quantity, Channel, PaymentMethod, Target, etc.
Covers sales data for 2026 (January, February, March)

4- Features & Highlights: 
KPI Cards — Monthly Revenue Breakdown-
 Displays Net Revenue for each month as individual KPI cards:

   January: ₹6.96M
   February: ₹13.37M
   March: ₹14.66M
   Total Q1 Net Revenue: ₹34.99M

Goal Tracker (KPI Visual)-
  Tracks Total Sales vs Target with a large KPI card
  Current achievement: ₹29.32M against a Goal of ₹6.8M — showing +331.17% over target
  Accompanied by an area chart showing monthly sales vs target trend

Sales by Channel (Donut / Pie Chart)-
  Quantity by Payment Method (Pie Chart)
  Shows customer payment preference distribution:
  EMI, Net Banking, Debit Card, UPI, Cash on Delivery, Credit Card

Top Products by Quantity Sold (Horizontal Bar Chart)
Daily Revenue Trend (Line Chart)-
  Line chart showing day-by-day Net Revenue fluctuation across the month
  Highlights peak and dip days, useful for spotting sales spikes or slow periods

Interactive Month Filter (Slicer)-
  A month slicer on the right panel lets users filter the entire dashboard by:
  Select All / Individual months (January – December)
  All visuals update dynamically based on selection
